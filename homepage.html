FAQ

Questions Sellers Ask Us

Do you charge fees or commissions?

No. There are no agent commissions, no hidden fees, and no obligation. The offer we present is what you receive, before any normal closing items.

Do I need to make repairs or clean out the property?

No. We buy as-is — vacant, partially occupied, or in need of work. You don't need to repair, stage, or show anything.

How quickly can I close?

On your timeline. If you need to move fast, we can work with that. If you need more time, we'll build the closing date around you.

What types of properties do you buy?

Single-family homes, multi-family and investment properties, plus commercial: office, retail, and strip centers.

Is my consultation confidential?

Yes. Every conversation is private and carries no obligation. Commercial sales can be handled quietly, without public marketing.

What if I'm not sure selling for cash is right for me?

That's exactly what the consultation is for. We'll walk through your options — including creative financing structures — so you can decide with clarity.
