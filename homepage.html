The Stakes

Cyber Attacks Cost More Than Data

A cyber incident does not just create technical problems. It can lead to lost customers, operational downtime, recovery costs, regulatory headaches, and long-term damage to your company’s reputation.

Costly Recovery

Malware removal and data recovery can be expensive—and the invoice keeps growing the longer an incident lingers.

Business Downtime

Downtime disrupts your team and customers, stalling revenue and eroding trust with the people who depend on you.

Reputation Damage

A damaged reputation can cost future business long after the immediate incident is resolved and systems are back online.

People at Risk

Vendors, employees, and customers may all be affected—the impact of one incident spreads well beyond your walls.
