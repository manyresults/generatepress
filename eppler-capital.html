§ VII. Investor Questions

Frequently asked questions.

A short reference for the questions we hear most often from investors evaluating an allocation with Eppler Capital.

Q. Who is eligible to invest with Eppler Capital Funds?

A. Our offerings are private placements available to accredited investors, as defined under Regulation D of the Securities Act. Prospective investors complete a suitability review and receive full offering documents prior to any commitment.

Q. How are distributions typically paid?

A. Distributions are paid monthly, direct-deposited to your bank of record. Timing and rate are outlined in each offering's private placement memorandum.

Q. What is the typical hold period?

A. Most of our current strategies target a hold period of five to seven years, matched to the operating cycle of the underlying real assets and businesses. Every offering discloses its target hold and liquidity terms.

Q. How do you conduct diligence on operators?

A. Every operator we partner with is subject to a comprehensive review of financials, track record, references, and on-site visits. Fewer than five percent of the operators we evaluate meet our standard.

Q. How do I begin the process?

A. The most useful starting point is a complimentary portfolio review. We'll walk through your current allocations, income needs, and objectives, and share whether — and where — a private allocation might fit.
