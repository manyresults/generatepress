Disclaimers

General Disclaimer

Participation in the CMI Contractor Partnership Program is subject to approval based on eligibility criteria, including verification of contractor status and alignment with program objectives. CMI reserves the right to modify or terminate program benefits, rewards, and requirements at any time without prior notice.

Discounted Pricing Disclaimer

Discounted pricing is available exclusively to approved partners and applies only to eligible products and services. Discounts may vary based on volume, project type, and other criteria outlined in the partnership agreement.

Co-Marketing Disclaimer

Co-marketing opportunities, including partner badges and promotional materials, are subject to CMI's approval and must adhere to brand guidelines. Misuse of CMI branding or co-marketing assets may result in termination of partnership privileges.

Priority Fulfillment Disclaimer

Priority fulfillment is available for approved projects and subject to material availability, current workload, and operational constraints. While CMI prioritizes partner projects, timelines may vary depending on order complexity and other factors.

Referral Program Disclaimer

Referral rewards are contingent upon the successful completion of referred projects and approval by CMI. Specific terms, including reward structure and eligibility, will be provided upon program enrollment. Fraudulent or duplicate referrals may result in disqualification.
