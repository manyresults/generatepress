Services Offered

Grove Wellness Center provides a range of services designed to support physical recovery, emotional wellness, functional movement, and ongoing personal development.

Acupuncture

Used to support balance, reduce pain or tension, calm the nervous system, and encourage the body's natural healing processes. Sessions are tailored to each person's needs and may be part of a broader wellness or rehabilitation plan.

Hypnosis

Offers focused support for mental, emotional, and behavioral patterns. It can help clients access deeper awareness, shift internal responses, and support change in a calm, guided setting.

Tui Na

A therapeutic bodywork modality within East Asian Medicine. May be used to address pain, tension, movement restriction, stress-related patterns, and recovery needs through skilled, hands-on care.

Physical Therapy and Rehabilitation

Support movement, strength, mobility, recovery, and return to function. Care may include assessment, movement training, functional rehabilitation, and education to help clients move with more confidence.

Cosmetic Acupuncture and Microneedling

Support skin health, circulation, and natural rejuvenation through an integrative wellness lens. Services are designed to honor the whole person while addressing cosmetic goals.

Individual Appointments

One-on-one care tailored to your needs, goals, and current stage of healing or development. Each appointment is approached with attention to the whole person.

Group Sessions

A supportive environment for shared practice, learning, and guided wellness work. Group sessions offer community connection alongside structured care.

Online Meditation

Accessible support for grounding, focus, stress reduction, and nervous system regulation — from wherever you are. Sessions are available online to support a consistent practice.

Online Qigong

Gentle movement, breath, and awareness practices designed to support balance, energy, mobility, and calm. Available online so you can participate from home or anywhere.

Community Clinic

A more accessible care option for people seeking support in a shared or community-centered setting. The community clinic extends Grove Wellness Center's mission to serve more people.
