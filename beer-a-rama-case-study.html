The Challenge

A High Effective Rate
and No Way to Verify It

Beer-A-Rama was paying a high effective rate on its credit card processing, but ownership had no clear way to evaluate whether the fees were competitive. Prior conversations with processors had been murky about what actually counted as a qualifying "savings" under audit terms, making it hard to trust any proposed switch.

On top of that, the store was hesitant to sign a long-term contract without understanding exactly what they'd be locked into and why. They needed an apples-to-apples audit, clear definitions of savings, and terms that only bind the business if real savings are actually delivered.

The Solution

Three Pricing Paths, Not a Take-It-or-Leave-It Offer

SPARQ conducted a full audit of Beer-A-Rama's payment processing and presented three distinct pricing scenarios so the client could compare real trade-offs across customer experience, effective rate, and contract exposure.

01 • Scenario

Dual Pricing

A 3% surcharge passed to all card transactions. Delivers the largest projected savings when customers absorb the processing cost.

Projected: $79,939.56 / yr

02 • Scenario

Surcharge-Only Pricing

A reduced debit rate paired with a surcharge model. A middle path that shares the processing cost between the business and the customer.

Projected: $23,188.92 / yr

03 • Scenario

Traditional Pricing

A lower effective processing rate with no surcharge structure. Preserves the current customer experience while still recovering meaningful margin.

Projected: $13,231.80 / yr

Client Protections

Savings You Can Trust, Terms That Only Bind if We Deliver

To address Beer-A-Rama's concerns about vague savings claims, SPARQ locked down two protections in writing before any change was made.

Savings excludes normal market rate drops. Only improvements SPARQ's changes actually deliver count toward the audit — so results can't be inflated by shifts in the broader processing market.

36-month term is conditional. The contract only takes effect if the proposed future savings are actually implemented, so Beer-A-Rama is never locked in without seeing results first.

The Results

Every Scenario Delivered Real, Recoverable Margin

The audit uncovered meaningful savings across all three scenarios, giving Beer-A-Rama the flexibility to choose the option that best fit their risk tolerance and customer experience preferences.

Even the most conservative option delivered over $13,000 in annual savings, while the top-performing dual pricing model projected nearly $80,000 per year back into the business — with contract protections in place to ensure Beer-A-Rama only pays for savings actually realized.
