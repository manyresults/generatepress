The Beats

What Makes a Good Tip?

We cover five beats, and all of them start with someone like you witnessing something and thinking "this cannot go unreported."

🚗 Traffic

The intersection that ruined your marriage. Roadwork that's been "almost done" since 2019.

🔪 Crime & Vice

Stolen catalytic converters, Wawa bathroom incidents, parole-energy classifieds.

🗺️ Regional

Croydon, Bristol, North Jersey slander, "not real Philly" discourse.

🎪 Culture

Disney adults, Facebook-group meltdowns, stepdads in the wild.

🌀 Chaos

Sightings, fake legislation, and apocalyptic nonsense delivered with a straight face.

🤫 You can stay anonymous.

Use a fake name, skip your contact info, or label yourself "a concerned resident." We protect our sources like they witnessed something at the diner.

👀 We read everything.

Not every tip runs, but every tip is reviewed by an actual human who also has to drive Woodbourne Rd.
