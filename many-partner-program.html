Common questions

How do I earn?

For Tier 1, no — MANY can assign system clients to you. For Tier 2 and 3, you’re building your own book. Tier 1 contributors with strong performance get first priority on inbound leads.

Do I need to bring my own clients?

Tier 1 is pure rev share — you earn a percentage of what you deliver and retain. Tier 2 and 3 are platform-fee models — you collect directly from your clients and keep the margin.

Can I build my own brand inside this program?

Yes. Tier 2 is designed exactly for that. You launch under your own name with MANY infrastructure behind you. When you’re ready to go fully independent, Tier 3 is the path.

Is this a job?

No. You’re an independent operator, not an employee. You’re accountable for outcomes, not activity. No titles, no hierarchy, no busywork.

How long before I’m earning?

Tier 1 contributors can earn from their first client. Tier 2 partners typically see their first client within 30–60 days of launch if they’re actively networking. There are no guarantees — this is performance-based at every level.

What’s the difference between tiers?

Tier 1: work inside MANY’s brand, earn rev share. Tier 2: build your own brand on MANY’s infrastructure, pay a platform fee. Tier 3: operate fully independently, pay a 10% MRR share.
