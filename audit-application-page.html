What You Receive

Exactly what you receive

We examine the information available, separate evidence from assumptions, and give you a practical plan you can use with your existing team, your vendors, or us.

1. Marketing & business snapshot

Your goals, audience, offer, current marketing mix, customer journey, and known performance measures. We flag missing data and the questions that prevent a reliable assessment.

2. Vendor accountability review

Up to two vendors. What each was engaged to do, the evidence of delivery, how reported results connect to your goals, plus gaps, duplicated work, unclear ownership, and questions to ask them.

3. Channel & customer journey review

Up to two priority channels (paid ads, SEO, social, email, or another agreed channel) and how they connect to your website, lead capture, and follow-up.

4. Confidential written findings

An executive summary, key observations, supporting evidence, information gaps, and recommended priorities. Where evidence is thin, we say so rather than guess.

5. 90-day action plan

A sequenced roadmap for days 1–30, 31–60, and 61–90, with recommended actions, suggested owners, and measures of progress.

6. 60-minute findings review

One live video call to walk through the report and plan, answer questions, and help you decide what to do next. Your team or advisors may join.

We won’t label a vendor ineffective or estimate savings without supporting evidence. Contract interpretation is a business review, not legal advice. The channel review is focused, not an exhaustive technical audit of every account or asset.

Process & Expectations

Clear scope. No surprises.

Process and timing

  • Apply: submit a short application and receive the link to book your complimentary consultation.
  • Consult: a free 15-minute conversation to confirm fit.
  • Protect & authorize: sign the audit agreement and mutual NDA, identify the vendors and channels, and pay the one-time fee.
  • Share: provide the agreed information and access.
  • Review: we review the selected areas and prepare your findings and action plan.
  • Deliver & discuss: written report in a target of 5–7 business days, then your 60-minute review call.

What we need from you

  • Choose the two vendors and two channels for review
  • A primary contact and your business goals
  • Recent reports and metrics, vendor scopes or agreements, and relevant invoices where available
  • Read-only access when practical, or exports and screenshots if not
  • Share only what you’re authorized to disclose. Never send passwords by ordinary email

We’ll identify missing materials and agree on how to proceed if they can’t be supplied. If information is delayed or incomplete, the timeline may shift and we’ll tell you the impact.

What is not included

  • Implementation, campaign or account management, and technical fixes
  • Vendor negotiations or direct vendor communications
  • Ongoing monitoring
  • Legal, accounting, cybersecurity, or regulatory advice
  • Reviews beyond the two agreed vendors and two channels

Additional work requires a separate written agreement and fee. We don’t guarantee revenue, savings, rankings, leads, or vendor performance.
