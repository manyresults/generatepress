Properties We're Glad to Hear About

Not Every Property Fits the MLS

Some properties are harder to sell the traditional way. If your client's situation is on this list, or just doesn't fit the usual mold, we're happy to take a look.

01

Needs Significant Repairs

Properties that need more work than the seller wants to take on before a conventional sale.

02

Water, Fire, or Structural Issues

Properties with damage or condition concerns that can complicate a traditional listing.

03

Expired or Fell-Through Listings

Listings that expired or fell out of contract, where the seller is looking for another path.

04

Estate or Inherited Properties

Heirs who want a simpler way to handle a property they didn't plan on owning.

05

Vacant, Tenanted, or Behind on Taxes

Properties with occupancy, tax, or other complications that make a standard sale harder.

06

Small Commercial Properties

Office, retail, and strip center properties, including those with vacancies.
