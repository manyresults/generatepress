Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pacioli Finance only work with businesses in these six industries?

These are our priority industries, but we work with any established small business generally in the $2.5M–$5M revenue range that needs controller-level accounting support.

Do you charge differently by industry?

No. Pacioli uses one flexible hourly model with a monthly minimum that scales with your needs, regardless of industry.

Can you support a business that spans more than one of these industries?

Yes. Many clients don't fit neatly into one category, and our engagements are scoped around your actual needs, not a fixed package.
