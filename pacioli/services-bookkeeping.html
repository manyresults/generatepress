Scope of Work

Full Service Breakdown

Accounts Receivable (AR)

Pacioli manages incoming payments, ensuring timely billing, payment application, and collection to optimize cash flow.

Generate and send accurate invoices, including complex, high-volume, or custom contracts

Monitor incoming payments (checks, ACH, wire transfers) and apply them to the correct accounts

Follow up on overdue invoices and communicate directly with customers to secure payment

Investigate and resolve payment discrepancies, deductions, and customer queries

Reconcile the AR ledger with the general ledger and maintain aging reports

Review customer credit risk and recommend credit holds or limit changes

Roles covered: Bookkeeper, AR Specialist, AR Analyst, Billing Specialist, Collections Specialist, Senior/Lead AR Accountant, Revenue Accountant, Financial Analyst

Accounts Payable (AP)

Pacioli manages short-term obligations by processing vendor invoices, verifying purchase orders, reconciling statements, and executing timely payments.

Receive, review, code, and match invoices to purchase orders and receiving documents

Schedule and prepare payments, including ACH transfers and check runs

Correspond with vendors, reconcile statements, and resolve billing discrepancies

Reconcile AP accounts, process expense reports, and assist with month/year-end closing

Maintain organized digital files for audit purposes, including W-9s and 1099 processing

Roles covered: Bookkeeper, AP Clerk/Specialist, AP Analyst, AP Supervisor/Lead, Accounts Payable Manager, Billing/Procurement Specialist, Treasury/AP Specialist

Fixed Assets & Depreciation

Pacioli manages the property, plant, and equipment (PPE) lifecycle — from acquisition through disposal — ensuring GAAP/IFRS compliance.

Track assets from acquisition to disposal, including additions, transfers, and retirements

Calculate and record monthly depreciation and intangible-asset amortization

Determine whether expenditures should be capitalized or expensed; manage Construction in Progress (CIP)

Reconcile the fixed asset register to the general ledger and prepare roll-forward schedules

Roles covered: Fixed Asset Clerk, Fixed Assets Accountant, Asset Accountant, Corporate Accountant, GL Accountant, Tax/Regulatory Accountant, Financial Controller, CMA

Budgeting & Forecasting

Pacioli creates, manages, and analyzes financial plans that translate strategic goals into actionable budgets.

Create annual budgets and 1–5 year long-term financial plans with department managers

Update revenue and expense projections on a rolling basis based on market and operational trends

Compare actuals against budget/forecast, analyze variances, and recommend corrective action

Roles covered: Budget Clerk, FP&A Analyst, Financial Analyst, Senior Financial Analyst, Budget Analyst, FP&A Manager, Finance Manager, Financial Controller, CMA

Month-End Close

Pacioli reconciles bank accounts, balances the general ledger, records journal entries, and prepares financial statements every month.

Match bank statements, credit cards, vendor invoices, and AR to the general ledger

Record monthly accruals, prepayments, and depreciation

Update inventory records and post payroll entries

Generate income statements and balance sheets, and lock the books after review

Roles covered: Bookkeeper, GL Accountant, Senior Accountant, AP/AR Clerk, Accounting Clerk, Accounting Manager, Financial Controller, CMA

Financial Reporting

Pacioli prepares, analyzes, and maintains income statements, balance sheets, and cash-flow statements — accurate, GAAP-compliant, and delivered on time.

Produce monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements under GAAP or IFRS

File reports with regulatory bodies (e.g., SEC) as applicable

Investigate and explain variances between actual results and forecasts/budgets

Roles covered: Bookkeeper, GL Accountant, Senior Accountant, Accounting Manager, Financial Controller, CMA, Financial Reporting Accountant, Corporate Accountant

Internal Controls & Compliance

Pacioli segregates duties among authorization, custody, recording, and reconciliation to reduce the risk of fraud and error.

Asset custody vs. recording: the person who handles cash/checks isn't the person who records the receipt

Authorization vs. execution: the person approving a purchase isn't the person paying it

Reconciliation vs. operations: the person reconciling bank statements doesn't initiate transactions

Design, implement, and monitor internal policies to meet legal and regulatory standards

Conduct routine internal audits and investigate reported irregularities

Roles covered: Internal Controls Clerk/Analyst, SOX Compliance Analyst, Compliance Officer, Audit Accountant/Internal Auditor, Financial Controller, CMA, IT/Finance Controls Specialist
