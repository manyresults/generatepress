More about the firm and the friar it's named for
Why “Pacioli”

Every entry has two sides.

Luca Pacioli was the Franciscan friar who documented double-entry bookkeeping in 1494 and worked alongside Leonardo da Vinci. Every balanced set of books since traces back to that one chapter.

We took the name because the principle still holds: every entry has two sides, and the truth shows up when they match.

A CMA is trained for the inside of a business: reporting, controls, analysis. A CPA is trained for the outside: attestation and returns. Most owners need both, at different moments.
