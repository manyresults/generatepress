Our Namesake

Inspired by Luca Pacioli, the Father of Accounting

In 1494, Luca Pacioli published a foundational section on bookkeeping in his Summa de Arithmetica, Geometria, Proportioni et Proportionalita, detailing the double-entry system — debits, credits, ledgers, and trial balances — that standardized accounting practices used by merchants and businesses across Europe. He also collaborated with Leonardo da Vinci on mathematical and accounting diagrams, illustrating the era’s blend of art, science, and commerce. Our name honors his legacy: accuracy, structure, and financial clarity.

Why “Pacioli”
