Property Tax Relief Most People Never Apply For

New Jersey runs several property tax relief programs, and they're routinely missed. If you own a home and you're 65 or older, a veteran, or on a fixed income, there's a decent chance you qualify for at least one. These are state programs, not ours. Inherited a House

Inherited a House in New Jersey? Start With the Surrogate

If someone died and the deed is still in their name, nothing can be sold or transferred until the estate is opened. In New Jersey that happens at the county Surrogate's office, in the county where the person lived. You don't always need a lawyer to open an estate, but you do need to go there first.

Two Things That Stall Inherited House Sales

The tax waiver

Before New Jersey real estate can be transferred out of a deceased person's name, the state Division of Taxation has to issue a waiver, Form 0-1. It's a routine step, but it has a queue, and closings get pushed back when nobody starts it early. Bank accounts and non-real-estate assets use a different form, the L-8.

Title problems nobody checked

Old mortgages, unpaid taxes, a prior heir who was never properly removed, a deed that was never recorded. These surface at the worst possible time. Ask the question early.

Free Legal Help

South Jersey Legal Services handles civil cases for low-income residents, including estate and housing matters, at offices in each county. Legal Services of New Jersey runs a statewide hotline at 1-888-576-5529.

Care & Aging Services

Your County Office on Aging Is the Front Door

Every New Jersey county has an Area Agency on Aging, and it also serves as the Aging & Disability Resource Connection. That's a long name for a simple thing: one office that can tell you what in-home care, caregiver support, meals, transportation, and long-term care options exist in your county and who pays for them. It's free and you don't have to be low-income to call.

Two Numbers Worth Saving

1-877-222-3737

The statewide Aging & Disability Resource Connection line, if you're not sure which county office you need.

2-1-1

New Jersey's information and referral line. Senior services, legal help, utility assistance, food. Available 24/7.

Medicare Questions

Free, unbiased counseling through the State Health Insurance Assistance Program. In Burlington and Camden, call Senior Citizens United Community Services at 856-456-1121. In the other five counties, ask your Office on Aging for the SHIP counselor.

Veterans

For Veterans and Surviving Spouses

Patriot is veteran-owned, and this is the section we care most about getting right. Two things veterans and widows routinely miss:

Your county Veterans Service Officer

Every South Jersey county has one. They file VA claims and appeals for you at no cost, and they know the local programs that stack on top of federal benefits.

VA Aid & Attendance

A pension benefit for wartime veterans and surviving spouses who need help with daily living. It can pay toward home care or assisted living, and it's the difference between staying home and not for a lot of families.
