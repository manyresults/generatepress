Meet the Clinicians

The practitioners at Grove Wellness Center bring focused training, deep curiosity, and genuine care to every session — combining wellness, acupuncture, hypnosis, and rehabilitation to help each person move, heal, and grow.

Dr. Charles Illingworth

LAc, DAc, DiplAc, CHT

Dr. Charles is deeply, passionately invested in assimilating the mysteries of your internal world. At every moment of your session, you are with someone who is completely present and fully committed to an understanding of who you are, and how you work.

As someone who is able to develop and maintain multiple perspectives on your issues, Charles has a gift for seeing connections that are easily missed, based on a blend of intuition, laser focus, and years of training. His goal is to move stuck emotions within you to clear the past, so that you can be fully present in your future. In his clinic, you will receive a compassionate witness to your suffering, a witness who is profoundly committed to shifting your course from suffering to freedom.

Dr. Stephanie Neuerburg

PT, DPT, LAc, DAc, Diplomate

Dr. Stephanie is a lifelong student of movement, starting as an athlete. She has been working for the last 11 years as a physical therapist specializing in functional movement training in the orthopedic and neurological populations, and recently as a licensed acupuncturist. Her goal is always to have the patient be able to return to elite performance. Dr. Neuerburg has specialized in running assessment and return to sport, including bio-mechanical movement optimization and functional movement training. Dr. Neuerburg is a constant student of the human body and continues her education not only in various training methodologies but also in healing practices including neurofunctional acupuncture.

In recent years, Dr. Neuerburg has opened her abilities to a top-down approach, which includes a more holistic approach and creating positive habits in everyday life. She accomplishes this by creating a full body analysis that allows for a complete picture of a patient's dysfunction to help restore natural movements and decrease painful responses. This creates a whole-body method of healing rather than just focusing on the injured area and its rehabilitation. Dr. Neuerburg's top-down approach creates the ability to heal the afflicted area in a way that overcomes and reintegrates the injury into a healthier movement dynamic. In doing so, Dr. Neuerburg will help you heal and prevent future injuries. By healing the whole, one can heal the parts and be in a better position for success than before an injury.

Janelle Farkas

Dipl. Ac., LAc

Janelle is a licensed acupuncturist specializing in Tui Na, a medical bodywork modality within East Asian Medicine. She integrates Tui Na and acupuncture to support recovery, reduce pain, and improve functional movement patterns. Her clinical focus includes motorsport athletes and automotive performance professionals; veterans and first responders; and individuals experiencing acute or chronic pain, tension, or stress-related movement restriction.

Before entering clinical practice, Janelle trained and worked as an engineer and spent more than a decade as a U.S. Army civilian, while actively involved in the performance automotive and racing community. This experience informs her work with patients who place high demands on their bodies, whether in sport, work, or service. Janelle's approach blends biomechanical understanding with the diagnostic frameworks of East Asian Medicine to help patients return to efficient movement, reduce compensatory patterns, and build resilience in the nervous system.
