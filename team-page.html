Meet Our Leadership Team

Managing Partner

Marc-André S. Buenger

Marc-André S. Buenger is a Trilingual European Institutional Equities professional with a demonstrated record of success marketing financial products in the U.S. and internationally.

Mr. Buenger has spent his career working for American (Merrill Lynch), British (ICAP), French (Natixis Bleichroeder), German (Westdeutsche Landesbank) and Swiss (UBS) investment banks specializing in equity sales and trading, serving U.S. based mutual funds, pension funds and hedge funds. While residing primarily in New York, Paris, Boston and, briefly, London, Mr. Buenger has created and built businesses on 5 different platforms in both finance and media. Mr. Buenger and Mr. Cordiano launched Quintet Partners in 2014, a cost solutions-expense reduction company.

Earlier in his career, Mr. Buenger worked for the Permanent German Mission to the United Nations as an Aide to the Assistant Attaché and served as an International Currency Trader and Analyst in New York for a Seoul, South Korea based investment corporation.

Education

Managing Partner

Ron Marvo

Ron Marvo brings a relationship-first approach to business development, payments, client experience, and growth strategy. As Managing Partner of SPARQ, Ron helps business owners identify practical ways to improve performance, reduce friction, uncover hidden money, and keep more of the money they make.

With over 10 years of experience in sales, leadership, coaching, and merchant services, Ron has spent his career building trusted partnerships with business owners across multiple industries. He understands the day-to-day pressures operators face and knows how to cut through complexity to find the opportunities that actually move the needle.

Ron's strength is in the relationship. He listens first, asks the right questions, and builds strategies around what matters most to each client. Whether it is uncovering savings in payment processing, identifying vendor misalignment, or simply helping a business owner see the full picture, Ron brings energy, accountability, and follow-through to every engagement.

Education

B.A. in Business Administration & Management – West Virginia University

Advisory Board Partner

Gene Cordiano

Gene Cordiano was an Executive Vice President and Divisional President of the international asset-based lending division for the Bank of New York. Mr. Cordiano managed their locations in the United Kingdom, Canada, and the USA.

Mr. Cordiano founded Global Venture Partners, a cost solutions-expense reduction company in February of 2002 with offices in New York City and Santa Monica, California. He was President and COO of GEAR Magazine which he successfully ran and sold in 2008, and President and COO of Zanella, an international upscale apparel company with factories in Italy and operations in the United States. Mr. Cordiano expanded Zanella's profitability and successfully sold the company in 2015, securing a significant multiple to the bottom line.

Education

B.S. in Finance – Davidson College
M.B.A. in Economics – NYU, Stern School of Business
