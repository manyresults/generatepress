The Story of Yuengling

D.G. Yuengling & Son has been brewing in Pottsville, Pennsylvania since 1829, which makes it America's oldest brewing company. Yuengling Traditional Lager is its flagship, and it's a Pennsylvania staple at cookouts, tailgates and corner bars alike.
