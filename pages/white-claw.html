The Story of White Claw

White Claw launched in 2016 and took off in the summer of 2019, turning hard seltzer into one of the biggest categories in the beverage world. Its simple formula — seltzer water, a gluten-free alcohol base and a touch of fruit flavor — is still the standard for RTD seltzers.
