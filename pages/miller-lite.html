The Story of Miller Lite

Miller Lite went national in 1975, and its "Great Taste, Less Filling" slogan helped turn light beer into a household category. Today it's brewed by Molson Coors and is still one of the most popular beers in America — and one of the most requested at our Levittown store.

What to Drink Miller Lite With

Miller Lite's crisp, easy-drinking profile goes with pizza, wings, burgers, hot dogs, soft pretzels and chips. For best flavor, serve it ice cold — we chill everything to 37°F daily, so it's ready the moment you walk out the door.
