Ground Sublease

Build on established ground.

BeeVee subleases select parcels of its owned land for tenants who want to build, place, or operate their own improvements — without buying the land outright. The ground stays BeeVee's; the tenant brings the use.

Good fits

  • Pad sites and freestanding kiosks
  • Parking, fleet, or trailer staging
  • Signage and telecom/antenna placement
  • Storage containers or modular units
  • Seasonal or temporary outdoor use (retail, events, lots)

How it works

  • Long-term ground lease (typically 10–20+ years), tenant-built improvements
  • Triple-net structure: tenant covers taxes, insurance, and maintenance on their improvements
  • Improvements revert to BeeVee at lease end, or are removed per agreement
  • Straightforward, attorney-drafted lease — no corporate layers to negotiate through

“Your use, our ground — a long-term lease with a local owner who’s been here for 24 years.”
