Frequently Asked Questions

How long are ground leases?

Ground leases are long-term — typically 10–20+ years — with tenant-built improvements.

Who pays taxes and insurance on a ground lease?

Leases are triple-net: the tenant covers taxes, insurance, and maintenance on their improvements.

Are storage terms flexible?

Yes. Storage is month-to-month or annual, under a simple written agreement with no long-term commitment required.

Does BeeVee really buy with no financing contingency?

Yes. BeeVee buys with capital in hand — income-producing real estate and selectively, small cash-flowing local businesses.
