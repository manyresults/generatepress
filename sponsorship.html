Why Sponsor

Invest in local athletes. Reach local families.

Iron Diamond House is proud to offer annual business sponsorship opportunities for local businesses that want to support young athletes while gaining year-round visibility in the community.

As a locally owned, female-owned indoor sports facility in Souderton, Iron Diamond House was built to give athletes — especially softball players and female athletes — a dedicated place to train, develop, compete, and belong. Sponsorship helps support that mission while connecting your business with local athletes, families, coaches, teams, and community members.

Why Sponsor Iron Diamond House?

More than visibility — a lasting local impact.

Your sponsorship puts your business in front of local families, athletes, visitors, coaches, and community supporters while helping Iron Diamond House continue building a strong home for youth sports.

By becoming a sponsor, you are helping create a place where athletes can:

  • Train year-round
  • Build confidence
  • Develop their skills
  • Work with coaches and teammates
  • Compete, grow, and belong
  • See that their dreams and goals matter

Iron Diamond House is more than a training facility. It is a community hub built around opportunity, development, confidence, and local support.

A Local Partnership With Purpose

Founded here. Built for here.

Iron Diamond House was founded by Crystalrae States, a Pennridge High School graduate, lifelong Perkasie resident, and Pennridge High School softball coach. The facility was created to give athletes a space that feels intentionally built for them — not a space they are simply given when someone else does not need it.

"Softball players deserve the opportunity to train, develop, and compete in an environment where they feel valued and where they can see themselves thriving."

— Crystalrae States, Founder

When your business sponsors Iron Diamond House, you are not just buying visibility. You are supporting a locally owned, female-owned business and investing in young athletes across Pennridge, Souderton, Perkasie, Lansdale, Bucks County, and surrounding communities.
