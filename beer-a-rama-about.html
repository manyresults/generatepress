About Beer-A-Rama

Family owned and operated by the LaRosa family since 1968, Beer-A-Rama has been the "Low Price Leader," proudly serving the residents of Lower Bucks County for nearly 60 years.

Two generations of neighbors serving neighbors — with cold beer, fair prices, and the kind of friendly, knowledgeable service that keeps Levittown coming back.

The Beer-A-Rama Philosophy

Our philosophy is simple: provide excellent customer service, every single visit. Our customers can count on us every time they walk through the door.

We work hard to keep our products fresh and low-priced all of the time, and to provide a clean, friendly environment for our guests to enjoy while they shop for their favorites.
