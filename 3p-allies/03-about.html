Why I do this work

A while back, we hired someone new. They were capable and eager, and within a week they were lost. Where do I find what’s due? Who do I hand this to? What happens after I finish my part? The honest answer was, “You’re just supposed to know.” Nothing was written down. The process lived in a few people’s heads, and it had worked fine — until we grew.

So I built the system: tasks created automatically and assigned to the right people, due dates tied to real deadlines, and one board where everyone could see what was due today and what was overdue. The new hire knew what to do. The rest of the team stopped keeping handwritten lists. And I could finally see the work without chasing it.

Growth is the stress test, and every growing organization finds its cracks.

That’s the work 3P Allies does. Growth is the stress test, and every growing organization finds its cracks. I help you find them and fix them with the people who do the work.
