About the MBA

Bringing Local Business & Community Together

The Morrisville Business Association (MBA) was founded in 1974 with the goal of bringing our businesses and the community together. The MBA mission is to promote our local businesses, support community non-profit and service organizations, and promote local events.

We are a collective of passionate local business owners, entrepreneurs, and professionals committed to promoting economic growth and advocating for the interests of our members.

Our Namesake

Robert Morris — Financier of the American Revolution

Our Borough is named for Robert Morris, famous as the “Financier of the American Revolution.” Becoming a successful merchant after coming from England in his teens, by 1775 Morris was the wealthiest man in the Thirteen Colonies.

After the start of the American Revolution, Morris used both his personal wealth and business reputation to fund the Continental Army, Marines, and Navy, and to purchase essential military supplies. In 1781 the Continental Congress named Morris the Superintendent of Finance in order to resolve and stabilize the country’s finances. Morris then formed the Bank of North America, thus enabling the United States to pay the overdue Continental Army salaries.

In 1787 Morris was selected as one of the delegates to the Philadelphia Convention, from where the Constitution and Bill of Rights were written and adopted. A fact unknown to most is that our Robert Morris (with Connecticut’s Roger Sherman) were the only two delegates to sign all three of America’s founding documents: the Declaration of Independence, the Articles of Confederation, and the Constitution of the United States.

In His Honour

The MBA maintains his statue at the Robert Morris Plaza and provides the seasonal decorations and maintenance. We also host a very popular annual car show, participate in Morrisville’s Memorial Day Parade and other local events, and offer a scholarship to a graduating Morrisville High School senior majoring in a business course of study.

MBA members are invited to post information about their business on our Facebook page, which is open for public viewing. Our website provides links to our members’ websites, where we hope you will shop. MBA membership is open to any business or business owner in the 19067 area.

Our Town

About Morrisville

Morrisville’s proud history includes being one of the regional trading outposts established by the famed Dutch West India Company—about the same time their Director Peter Minuit purchased Manhattan from the indigenous Lenape and founded New Amsterdam, which later became New York. Trading primarily in furs, the population grew as vessels from Holland, Scandinavia, and England brought settlers and manufactured goods to this area, while returning with furs and foodstuffs.

Known initially as Colvin’s Ferry, Morrisville was first settled in 1624. Morrisville is home to Greystones, the historic site where in 1684 William Penn, the first Governor of Pennsylvania, confirmed the first Walking Purchase of land from the Lenape.

As the influx of settlers grew, so did the area, and Morrisville played a significant part in the American Revolution. Our historic Summerseat mansion was used by General George Washington as he planned the historic march on Trenton, Christmas Day 1776, which embarked from what is now Washington Crossing, PA. Summerseat was jointly owned by Morris along with two signers of both the Declaration of Independence and the U.S. Constitution: Morrisville’s George Clymer and Philadelphia’s Thomas Barclay. Barclay was born in Morrisville, so our town has a direct link to three of the signatories of the Declaration of Independence.

Today Morrisville has a population of some 8,600 people living within our 1.95 square miles. We are situated midway between New York City, NY, and Philadelphia, PA, along the banks of the Delaware River. The historic Delaware and Lehigh State Park Canal flows through Morrisville. Once a major waterway for transporting iron and coal from the Easton area to Bristol and the open river, today the Canal’s towpath is ideal for hikers, cyclists, joggers, canoers, and walkers. Both of these waterways are also favorite spots for fishermen.

Did You Know?

Fun Facts About Morrisville

A small borough with an outsized place in American history.

Almost the First Capital

Following the founding of the United States, Robert Morris pushed for Morrisville to be named the nation’s first capital—but it lost out to Philadelphia.

World Series Champions

In 1955 the Morrisville Little League Baseball Team won the Little League World Series title—one of only four Pennsylvania towns to have won the tournament since its 1947 inception.

America’s First Consul

Thomas Barclay, born in Morrisville, was America’s first Consul to France.
