FAQ

SEO, AEO and GEO: Straight Answers

What's the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO gets your website ranked in search results. AEO gets your answer pulled directly into featured snippets, voice search and AI Overviews. GEO gets your business mentioned and cited inside AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. All three run on the same foundation: a credible, consistent, well-documented business.

Is AEO or GEO replacing SEO?

No. AI tools still lean on search indexes and the open web to find and verify sources. Strong SEO is the foundation AEO and GEO are built on. Drop SEO to chase AI and you lose both.

Do I need a separate agency for AI search?

No. Splitting SEO and AI visibility between vendors creates conflicting signals about your business. We run all three as one system, under one strategy, with one report.

What does PR have to do with SEO and AI search?

Search engines and AI tools trust businesses that other credible sources talk about. Earned mentions in local press, industry publications, podcasts and associations are among the strongest authority signals you can build. We pitch real stories with real news value. We don't buy placements on pay-to-play "as seen on" networks.

Why is content marketing part of an SEO engagement?

Because content is what gets ranked, quoted and cited. Every article, guide and FAQ we write answers a question your buyers actually ask, leads with the answer, and carries a real author's name. Content without that discipline is just volume.

Can you guarantee my business will appear in ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews?

No, and no honest agency can. Nobody controls what AI tools say. What we control is making your business the clearest, most consistent and most credible source on your topic, which is exactly what these tools are built to find.

Does schema markup get me into AI answers?

Not by itself. Google has said no special structured data is required to appear in AI Overviews, and it stopped showing FAQ rich results in May 2026. We still use schema because it helps machines read your business accurately. We use it to describe the page truthfully, never as a trick.

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes and listing cleanup often show movement within weeks. Meaningful ranking and lead growth typically takes three to six months. Authority and AI citations compound over six to twelve months and beyond. Anyone promising overnight results is cutting a corner you'll pay for later.

What does white-hat SEO mean at MANY?

We only use methods search engines publicly support: technical fixes, genuinely useful content, accurate structured data, consistent listings, and links earned through real relationships and press. No bought links, no fake reviews, no hidden text, no mass-produced AI content.

How do you measure visibility in AI tools?

We track rankings and traffic in Google Search Console and GA4, including referral visits from AI assistants. Each month we also run your most important buyer questions through the major AI tools and log whether your business is mentioned, cited or missing.

Do you handle Google Business Profile and local SEO?

Yes. Google Business Profile, map pack visibility, local citations and review strategy are core to what we do for local and service-area businesses across the Philadelphia region and beyond.

Should I block AI crawlers from my website?

It's your call, and we'll walk you through the trade-offs. Blocking AI crawlers can protect content you'd rather not have reused, but tools that can't read your site can't recommend you either. For most service businesses, being readable is worth more.
