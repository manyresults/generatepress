The Challenge

Passion Alone Isn't Enough to Create Change

Advocacy organizations continue to struggle with stagnating membership, tight finances, and a workload that falls on too few people. Without the right framework, your efforts stay scattered and the change you're fighting for remains out of reach.

The SACRED Elements

Six Pillars of Every Successful Movement

Self-Reflection

Clarify your organization's core purpose and define the specific, unjust problem you will rally around.

Affiliation

Identify who joins in to work on this purpose with you and build a community of committed supporters.

Collaboration

Work across organizations to build collective power and amplify your impact beyond what any single group can achieve.

Recruiting

Bring new members into the organization to grow your base and distribute the workload across more committed hands.

Education

Provide information on the issue, the available solutions, and clear guidance on what people can do about it.

Dialogue

Understand differences and uncover commonalities to bridge divides and build broader coalitions for change.

The 4-Step Plan of Action

From Framework to Action

1

Identify the Problem

What specific, unjust problem will you rally around? How do you define it?

2

Identify the Solution

What options exist to solve the problem? Which option will you advocate?

3

Identify Who Can Make It Happen

Who holds the power? What are the “pillars of support” you need to reach?

4

Apply Pressure

How will you move the solution forward using Affiliation, Collaboration, Recruiting, Education, and Dialogue?
