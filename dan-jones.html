The Real Me?

Dan Jones, known as The Multipreneur Marketer, is a results-driven entrepreneur with a talent for building systems that deliver measurable impact. As the founder of Marketing Agency Near You (ManyResults.com), Dan has dedicated his career to helping businesses grow through innovative branding, strategic positioning, and effective lead generation.

With a slightly contrarian edge, Dan challenges traditional thinking to spark fresh ideas while maintaining an inclusive and collaborative approach. His ability to bring people together, paired with his sharp focus on long-term success, has made him a trusted partner for serial entrepreneurs and ambitious ventures alike.

Beyond his work with clients, Dan cohosts the Fire Your Boss Podcast, offering no-nonsense insights and candid discussions on modern business challenges. When he's not podcasting or crafting high-impact marketing strategies, you'll find him on the ice—balancing time between no fewer than four beer-league teams at any given moment.
