What is the Legacy Builder Academy?

The Legacy Builder Academy is a coaching program and community for residential real estate investors who are ready to layer in commercial — NNN, multifamily, industrial, self-storage, and more — without abandoning what's already working. You'll get direct access to Joe Evangelisti, a proven deal review process, and a community of serious operators doing exactly what you're trying to do.

Weekly Group Coaching

Live calls with Joe every week. Bring your deals, your questions, your stuck points. Get real answers from someone who's done it at scale — not recycled frameworks.

Live Money Lab

Monthly deep-dives on deal structuring, underwriting, capital stacks, and financing. This is where you sharpen your numbers skills and build confidence to pursue bigger deals.

Annual In-Person Event

Once a year, we gather in person. Masterminds, deal tours, relationship-building, and the kind of conversations that change your trajectory. Not a conference — a convening of operators.
