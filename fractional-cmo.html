FAQ

How is this different from an agency?

An agency does the work. We run the system that makes work happen — and decide what work is actually worth doing. (The CEO's CMO tier can also manage your existing agency.)

Why a 90-day commitment?

Because nothing meaningful happens in 30 days. The first 30 is installation. The next 60 is when the system starts compounding. After 90, you can cancel month-to-month with 30 days' notice — but most clients don't.

Can I upgrade tiers later?

Yes. Plenty of clients start at The Founder's CMO and graduate to The Operator's CMO once their team's built. Same engine, more ownership.

What if I don't fit cleanly into a tier?

Apply anyway. The application is also a diagnostic — if you're between tiers, we'll tell you which one fits. If neither does, we'll say so. We don't take clients we can't help.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Month one is your installation. No retainers, no hidden onboarding fee.

Do you take equity instead of cash?

No.
