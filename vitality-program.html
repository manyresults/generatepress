Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the membership replace my insurance?

No. Your office and telemedicine visits are billed to your insurance exactly as they are now. The membership covers the program around your care — your written plans, year-round structure, and progress tracking.

Do I have to come into the office?

Only if you want to. Most of the program works well by telemedicine, but in-person visits are available in Sparta whenever you prefer.

Can I just book regular visits instead of joining?

Absolutely. You're always welcome to be seen as a regular patient. The membership is for women who want a structured, proactive, year-round plan rather than visit-by-visit care.
