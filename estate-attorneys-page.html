Where We May Be Able to Help

Real Estate Can Complicate a Matter

Properties in estate and related matters can present challenges. For some clients, a direct sale as-is may be worth considering alongside other options. Whether it's appropriate is for you and your client to decide.

01

Multiple Heirs or Beneficiaries

Different goals and timelines can make agreeing on a sale difficult.

02

Vacant or Hard-to-Maintain Properties

Properties that are empty, deteriorating, or difficult for a fiduciary to manage.

03

Carrying Costs, Taxes, or Liens

Costs that can accumulate while a matter is pending.

04

Repairs Needed Before a Conventional Sale

Properties that would need work before they could be listed.

05

Tenant-Occupied Properties

Occupied properties that can complicate showings and a traditional sale.

06

Out-of-State Beneficiaries

Beneficiaries who live far away and want a more streamlined process.

FAQ

Questions Attorneys Ask Us

Is there a cost or obligation to refer a property?

No. Sending information doesn't obligate you or your client.

Does Patriot provide legal advice?

No. We're a buyer, not a law firm. Clients should rely on their own counsel.

Can a sale happen while a matter is still open?

That depends on the facts and on the authority and approvals required. We'll defer to you and the title company.

Do you consider properties with liens, back taxes, or title issues?

It depends on the specifics. Send us the details and we'll tell you whether we can help.

What happens to the information I send?

It's used to evaluate the property and follow up with you. Please see our privacy policy for details, and please don't include privileged or sensitive client information beyond what's needed.
