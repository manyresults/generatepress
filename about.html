Our Story

A home for athletes, families, coaches, and community.

Iron Diamond House is a locally owned, female-owned indoor youth sports facility in Souderton, Pennsylvania, built to give athletes a dedicated place to train, compete, grow, and belong.

Created with softball and female athletes at its core, Iron Diamond House provides a training environment where players can get the reps they need, build confidence, develop their skills, and feel valued every time they walk through the doors. With seven retractable batting tunnels, approximately 6,500 square feet of turf, clear-span training space, and high ceilings, IDH was designed for individual athletes, teams, coaches, camps, clinics, cage rentals, and special events.

But Iron Diamond House is more than a facility. It is a home for athletes, families, coaches, and the local sports community — a place where young athletes can see themselves thriving, where teams can come together, and where the next generation can chase big goals with the support of people who believe in them.

Built for Athletes

Built for athletes who deserve their own space.

Iron Diamond House was created to offer something different. Too often, softball players and female athletes have had to work around schedules, spaces, and systems that were not built with them in mind. Iron Diamond House was built to change that — to give athletes a space that feels intentional, welcoming, and made for their development.

"Softball players deserve the opportunity to train, develop, and compete in an environment where they feel valued and where they can see themselves thriving."

— Crystalrae States, Founder

At IDH, athletes are not an afterthought. They are the reason the facility exists.

Meet the Founder

About Crystalrae States

Iron Diamond House was founded by Crystalrae States — a Pennridge High School graduate, lifelong Perkasie resident, Pennridge High School softball coach, wife, mom of three, and lifelong sports enthusiast.

For Crystalrae, Iron Diamond House is personal.

She grew up in the community, played here, coaches here, and has spent years seeing firsthand how much sports can shape confidence, character, relationships, and opportunity. As both an athlete and a coach, she understood the need for a dedicated indoor training space where softball players and young female athletes could feel prioritized, supported, and proud to train.

"I wanted to create a place where female athletes could have a space that felt like it was built for them, not a space they were simply given when someone else didn't need it."

— Crystalrae States

What began as a lifelong dream has become a place built for the athletes Crystalrae has coached, cheered for, and believed in — especially the girls who deserve to know that their goals matter and their space matters.

Time for a more formal introduction: Crystalrae is the gal behind Iron Diamond House. She created IDH because she wanted female athletes to have a training home that was built for them from the beginning. This dream has been years in the making, and she cannot wait to pack the house with athletes, teams, families, coaches, and community members who believe in what this place stands for.

To all the girls: this one is for you. Chase your dreams, work hard, and bring your passions to life.

Community

Rooted in the local community.

Iron Diamond House is also an investment in the community Crystalrae calls home.

Located at 731 Wambold Road in Souderton, IDH serves athletes and families throughout Pennridge, Souderton, Perkasie, Lansdale, Bucks County, and surrounding communities. The facility was built to support local athletes, local teams, local coaches, and families looking for a high-quality indoor space close to home.

"I grew up here. I played here. I coach here. I know what this community means to me, and I wanted to build something that gives back to it. Iron Diamond House is about creating opportunities for the next generation and giving athletes a place where they know they belong."

— Crystalrae States

While softball is at the heart of Iron Diamond House, the mission reaches beyond one sport. IDH is a gathering place for young athletes, families, coaches, and the broader sports community — a place to train, celebrate, connect, and grow.

Our Mission

A dedicated indoor training home.

Our mission is to give athletes a dedicated indoor training home where they can build skills, confidence, relationships, and a lifelong love of sports.

Through training, competition, teamwork, and community, Iron Diamond House helps athletes grow both on and off the field. Whether an athlete is stepping into the cage for extra reps, joining a team practice, attending a clinic, or walking in for the first time, we want every player to feel welcomed, encouraged, and ready to work.
